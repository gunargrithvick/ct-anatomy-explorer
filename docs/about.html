About CT Anatomy Explorer

Teaching use only. This website is not for diagnosis or patient care.

What it is

A free teaching website for CT anatomy. On 5 public CT scans, two outlines are shown for each of 12 abdominal organs: one drawn by experts and one made by an AI model. Students compare the two outlines, read a short note on each organ and test themselves with a quiz.

How it was made

  1. The scans and the expert outlines come from the TotalSegmentator small dataset (v2.0.1, Zenodo record 10047263).
  2. The AI outlines were made with the VISTA-3D model (MONAI bundle, version 0.5.11) on a free Kaggle GPU.
  3. Each AI outline was compared with the expert outline: Dice score and organ volume.
  4. 10 scans were processed; the 5 clearest are shown. They were made smaller (2 mm) so that they load fast; the viewer uses NiiVue.
  5. The organ notes and the quiz are drafts; they will be checked by faculty on 27 Oct 2026.

What the Dice score means

Dice tells how much two outlines overlap: 1.00 means they are the same, 0 means they do not touch. The table for every organ is on the project page.

Limits

  • Only 5 scans are shown. They come from a public research dataset, not from our hospital.
  • Expert outlines are not perfect either: a low Dice can be an AI error or an outline error.
  • The scans were made smaller (2 mm), so small details are lost.
  • The AI model may be used for research and teaching only (NVIDIA licence, not for commercial use).

Credits and how to cite
