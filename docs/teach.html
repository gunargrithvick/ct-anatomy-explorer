Teacher's guide: one 45-minute class

Teaching use only – not for diagnosis.

Learning objectives

  1. Name and find 12 abdominal organs on axial and coronal CT.
  2. Describe where each organ lies in relation to its neighbours.
  3. Explain what an AI outline (segmentation) is and how the Dice score compares two outlines.
  4. Show, with an example, where an AI outline can be wrong.

Before the class

  • The website link is shared (a QR code on the first slide). One device for every two students; phones work.
  • The quiz needs no login. The worksheet below is printed (Ctrl + P prints only the worksheet).
  • Student feedback form: open the form (2 minutes, no names).

Answer notes for the teacher

  1. Axial images are seen from the feet, so the liver (patient's right) is on the left of the image and the spleen on the right.
  2. For example: the duodenum around the head, the stomach in front, the spleen near the tail; behind it the inferior vena cava, the aorta and the left kidney.
  3. The liver is usually the largest; the smallest depends on the scan.
  4. Answers differ; a good answer names the place (edge, tip, or a neighbouring organ).
  5. Kidneys, pancreas (mostly), duodenum (mostly), aorta, inferior vena cava.

Student worksheet – CT Anatomy Explorer

Name: ____________________ Date: ____________ Scan used: ______

  1. Scan 1, Expert view, axial: find the liver and the spleen. On which side of the image is each one? Why?
  2. Find the pancreas. Name two organs or vessels that lie next to it.
  3. In the table, which organ has the largest volume? Which has the smallest?
  4. Which organ has the lowest Dice in this scan? In Compare view, where do the AI and the expert disagree?
  5. Name three organs from the list that lie behind the peritoneum (retroperitoneal).
  6. Quiz score (one round): ____ / 10. Which organ did you miss?
